What you’ll learn and practise

Decisions you can apply to your own project.

Reading gives you the process. The result comes from practising it on your own idea, with the tools you choose.

  • Turn a broad idea into a deliberate visual direction and shot plan.
  • Build a moodboard and named recipes that keep a project consistent.
  • Hold one character across shots with a character sheet.
  • Choose lens, movement and light for a purpose — and plan coverage that cuts together.
  • Build a rough cut, match colour, integrate graphics and mix sound.
  • Deliver in the right format — and document your decisions for the next project.

Beginner questions

Before you buy.

I’m a complete beginner. Where should I start?

Start with Vol 01. It assumes no filmmaking background and teaches how to plan a project before you generate anything.

No. These are downloadable PDF books with images, written breakdowns and worked examples.

A PDF reader is enough to read the books. To practise, use the image, video and editing tools you prefer. The Complete Edition project files need Adobe After Effects and Premiere Pro to open fully. Software and subscriptions are not included.

Which option includes the editable project files?

Only the Complete Edition. The individual volumes include the PDF and their own bonuses.

Will this get me clients or a job?

No book can promise that. These books teach a process for making deliberate creative decisions; what you make with it depends on your practice.
